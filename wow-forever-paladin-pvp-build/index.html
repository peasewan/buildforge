PvP Build Goals

A PvP setup balances offensive pressure with defensive tools and utility. Plan enough damage to use short openings, enough survivability to answer enemy bursts, and enough flexibility to adapt to different opponents.

Popular PvP Variants

Retribution PvP emphasizes offensive pressure. Holy PvP focuses on healing and teammate support. A hybrid PvP path trades a deep specialization for selected tools across more than one talent tree.
