## Reddit / community feedback request
Title: I built a Paladin point-budget comparison tool — feedback on the route workflow?

I maintain BuildForgeTools. I added a small Paladin comparator that replays a Ret community route and a Holy Shock planning example from level 10 to 30. It shows each point, the next talent and a link to edit that exact allocation.

One useful limit it exposes: in our preserved 69913 tree, Holy Shock fits 21 Holy points, while Twist of Light needs 31 Ret points. The page clearly labels that snapshot boundary; it is not a current-client DPS ranking or an optimized Shockadin recommendation.

If you use talent planners, is the point-by-point route or the patch-to-build impact view more useful? Reports with a client build number and screenshots are especially helpful for reviewing the remaining data gaps.
