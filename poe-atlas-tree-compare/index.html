A 25-node 3.29 Atlas tree in 3.30

In a published SSF race allocation, two of the 25 selected IDs are retained, 20 change and three disappear. Eighteen direct connections between selected nodes are lost. A connection can disappear even when both node IDs survive.

Load the allocation, expand the affected pairs and check which side of a broken connection still does what you want. Then open the new tree to find a route. The 18 lost connections do not tell you how many refund points to spend.

What counts as a change?

A retained ID has the same name, effect lines, type, granted passive points and immediate connections. A changed ID differs in at least one of those fields. A removed ID may have a similar effect elsewhere; it is not automatically replaced. A retained node can still be unreachable through the rest of your old route.

For example, Yield Upon Yield (#2378) keeps its ID but its ore-deposit effect changes. Open its comparison to read both versions. This page checks individual nodes, not a legal route or a refund budget.
